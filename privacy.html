Privacy Policy

Draft for internal company review · 13 October 2026
Internal draft—not a guarantee of compliance. Confirm the legal entity, internal contact, retention periods and vendor agreements before deploying to employees.

1. Data we process

Employee identifiers, names, work email addresses, role/active status, authentication session data and report upload metadata (filename, upload time, file size and linked account). Reports may include inventory records, item codes, vendors, GRN references, stock balances, sales and indents. Employees should avoid unnecessary personal data in workbooks.

2. Purpose

We process these records to authenticate authorized employees, display dashboards, enable stock and sales analysis, maintain report versions, control access and support operational troubleshooting.

3. Where reports are stored

The dashboard's static HTML, CSS and JavaScript may be hosted on GitHub Pages. Excel reports are intended to be uploaded through the dashboard into private Supabase Storage buckets, with report metadata in a Supabase PostgreSQL database. Real business reports should not be committed to the public GitHub repository.

4. Who can access reports

Active staff may view administrator-published official store reports. Employee-uploaded private workbooks are restricted by account-specific access policies. Administrators can see cloud usage and manage official uploads. Application owners or infrastructure operators may have privileged system access in accordance with their roles. Access controls must be audited before production rollout.

5. Authentication and passwords

Supabase Auth validates credentials. Passwords are not intended to be stored inside public JavaScript or report spreadsheets. Employees can request a reset link at their registered email address, or change passwords while signed in. Do not share reset links.

6. How long data is kept

The current replacement design retains the active report for each report type and attempts to delete superseded Excel files after successful replacement; basic version metadata may remain. This deletion is asynchronous and can occasionally require cleanup. Final retention periods and account deletion processes must be approved by management.

7. Security measures and limitations

Private storage buckets, authenticated access, employee-linked policies, administrator roles, HTTPS and server-side password verification are intended safeguards. No system is risk-free. Browser session tokens and temporary file parsing occur on employee devices. Do not use shared computers without signing out.

8. Service providers and data location

The database project currently runs in Supabase's Mumbai region. Supporting authentication, delivery, hosting or operations may involve other service-provider systems and locations. Confirm vendor agreements, data transfers and company requirements before production use.

9. Employee rights and requests

For access, correction, account deactivation, password recovery, report deletion or suspected misuse, contact the designated company administrator. Requests will be evaluated according to applicable law and company policy.

10. Updates

This policy may be updated as the dashboard evolves. Staff should be informed of material changes. A final version should identify the responsible legal entity, contact details, effective date and approval authority.
