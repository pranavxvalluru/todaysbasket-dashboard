Privacy Policy

How the store dashboard is designed to handle employee accounts, operational workbooks and related metadata.

At a glance. This notice explains what employee and store information is used, where it is stored, who may access it and how to raise a concern.

01. Who is responsible for the data

This draft describes the proposed handling of information within the Today's Basket Store Dashboard & Analysis. The responsible legal entity, designated business contact and grievance or privacy contact must be confirmed by the operating company before employee rollout. This document is an internal transparency notice and is not a claim of full legal compliance.

02. Information collected and processed

The Platform may process employee names, employee IDs, account-linked email addresses, job titles, access roles, active status, login and recovery events, report filenames, timestamps, file sizes, uploaded-by references and operational workbooks. Report content may include products, vendors, GRN references, batches, expiry dates, sales, prices, stock availability and pending indents. Employees should not upload unrelated personal data or credentials.

03. Why this information is used

The intended purposes are to verify employee identity, authorize appropriate features, load and analyze permitted reports, support stock and sales decisions, maintain current versions and limited upload history, detect misuse, troubleshoot failures and administer service security. Business reports must not be used for unrelated marketing or personnel profiling without a separate lawful basis and company authorization.

04. Where information is stored and processed

The public-facing website may serve only HTML, CSS and JavaScript from GitHub Pages. Secure sign-in is handled through Supabase Auth. Private Excel workbooks are intended to be held in non-public Supabase Storage buckets, with report metadata in the project database. Browser memory is used to parse and display files, and session data may be cached on the employee's device. Never publish confidential workbooks to the GitHub Pages repository.

05. Who has access

Official reports are intended for active, authorized employees, subject to role-based controls. Employee personal workbooks are intended to be accessible only to the uploading employee. Administrators may manage official reports and access certain system usage information. Infrastructure personnel and service providers may have limited privileged access as needed to operate and secure the service; administrative access should be logged and reviewed.

06. Security safeguards

Current design safeguards include HTTPS, Supabase Auth, access tokens, private storage buckets, database Row Level Security policies, scoped employee folders, administrative publishing restrictions, and backend password checks. Permissions should be verified against real employee and administrator accounts before rollout. No technology guarantees absolute security. Accounts, backups and service configurations require ongoing maintenance and review.

07. Report replacement and retention

When a current report is replaced, the Platform is intended to activate the new version before removing the superseded stored workbook. Cleanup can fail or take time; small audit and version records may remain even after workbook removal. The company must determine approved retention periods for report files, operational logs, backups and former-employee accounts. Data cannot be assumed deleted everywhere merely because it no longer appears in the dashboard.

08. Access, correction and requests

Employees may contact the designated organization representative to request correction of their account information, access to relevant personal information, account deactivation, or review of a report that was uploaded in error. Requests will be handled under applicable law, valid employment requirements and the company's approved retention obligations. Password-reset links are sent to registered addresses and should not be forwarded.

09. Providers, location and international transfer

The Supabase project currently operates in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region; this does not establish that every authentication, email, CDN, support or backup operation remains in India. GitHub Pages and other infrastructure or email providers may process metadata elsewhere. Vendor terms, cross-border transfers and security responsibilities should be reviewed before processing live company-sensitive information.

10. Browser storage and cookies

The Platform may store authentication sessions and transient preferences in the browser, and may retain limited local history or caches on a device for usability. Employees should sign out and avoid using shared computers for confidential work. If the company later introduces analytics or additional tracking, this notice should be updated to explain the technologies and any required choices.

11. Incidents, disclosures and safeguards review

If employees suspect account compromise, incorrect permissions or inadvertent disclosure, they should promptly notify the authorized administrator. The organization should maintain an incident-response procedure, assess whether notifications are legally required, and review affected credentials, access policies and stored reports. We do not claim that incidents can never occur.

12. Policy updates and effective date

Material changes to data categories, purposes, vendors, access or retention should be reviewed and reflected in this policy before implementation. An approved version should carry an effective date, version, responsible legal entity, grievance contact and documented management approval.

13. Privacy questions and contact

For access, correction, deletion requests or privacy concerns, staff should use the company-approved internal contact channel. The responsible entity and named contact details are pending company confirmation.
