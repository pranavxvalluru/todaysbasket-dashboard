Terms & Conditions

Draft for internal company review · 13 October 2026
Internal draft. This is a proposed staff-use policy. It should be approved by Today's Basket / its operating company before being adopted as binding terms.

1. Who may use the dashboard

The Today's Basket Store Dashboard & Analysis is intended for authorized employees and approved administrators. Access is granted through individual employee accounts, not shared passwords.

2. Permitted use

Use the dashboard only for legitimate store operations, including stock, sales, GRN, indent and related performance analysis. Follow company policies for handling confidential business information.

3. Account responsibilities

Protect your password, do not share account access, and report suspected misuse or accidental disclosure to the store administrator promptly. Company administrators may suspend accounts when employees leave or access is no longer appropriate.

4. Report accuracy and decisions

Analysis depends on the completeness, freshness and correctness of uploaded reports. Check report dates and source files before taking operational or financial decisions. Inventory snapshots must not be interpreted automatically as original GRN receipt quantities.

5. Uploading files

Upload only business-authorized Excel reports. Employees may upload private working copies, while only designated administrators may publish official store reports. Do not upload passwords, bank statements, unrelated personal information or files without permission.

6. Data ownership and access

Uploaded company records remain company information. Personal working copies are restricted to the relevant employee account in the current system design; official published reports are available to authorized active staff.

7. Availability and changes

The application is still being developed. Data import, calculations and availability may be interrupted. Features and report formats may be updated after testing and notice to employees.

8. Security and incident reporting

Do not bypass access controls, attempt to access another employee's files or publish report URLs externally. Inform the designated administrator if you suspect a breach or incorrect permission.

9. Retention and deletion

New report versions may replace older stored Excel files after a successful upload. Basic upload history may be retained for audit purposes. Company-approved retention and deletion schedules should be set before production use.

10. Contact and approval

For access requests, password issues or report corrections, contact your authorized Today's Basket system administrator through the company's established internal channel. These terms require management/legal review.
