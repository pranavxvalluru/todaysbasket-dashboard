01. Scope, status and acceptance

These Terms & Conditions govern access to the Today's Basket Store Dashboard & Analysis (the “Platform”), a restricted internal application used by authorized staff. They apply alongside employment contracts, information-security rules, and other company policies. In the event of inconsistency, applicable law and formally approved company policies take precedence. These terms are a draft and are not binding company policy until approved and issued by the responsible entity.

02. Eligibility, authorization and roles

Only individuals expressly authorized by the organization may use the Platform. Each person must use their assigned employee ID or username and a separate password. Roles such as administrator, manager and employee determine access to features and files; a job designation does not automatically grant administrator privileges. Access may be modified, suspended or revoked upon a role change, security concern or end of employment.

03. Account and device responsibilities

Users must keep passwords and recovery links confidential, use strong unique credentials, sign out of shared computers, protect any exported spreadsheets and promptly report lost devices or suspicious account activity. Users must not impersonate others, share accounts, defeat security controls or attempt to access another employee's private workbooks. Password recovery should use only the registered company-approved address.

04. Permitted use and prohibited activity

The Platform may be used for legitimate store operations such as viewing sales, assessing stock requirements, reviewing GRNs, performing indent planning and tracking approved reports. Prohibited conduct includes unauthorized exports or redistribution, scraping outside business purposes, uploading malware, manipulating records to mislead colleagues, and disclosing supplier, pricing, staffing or sales information to unauthorized persons. Access to reports is not authorization to publish them externally.

05. Reports, uploads and classification

Users must upload only accurate, lawful and business-authorized Excel or CSV files. Files may contain confidential commercial information; only the minimum information necessary for an approved task should be included. Administrators may publish official report versions for permitted staff. Employee working copies are intended to remain scoped to the employee account. A new upload can supersede an older one, but a successful upload does not by itself verify the business accuracy of its contents.

06. Calculations and management approvals

The Platform provides decision-support calculations, not a substitute for store controls. Before placing orders, approving indents, posting GRNs, applying discounts or making financial decisions, users must verify source dates, SKU mapping, units of measure, pending-quantity expiry, warehouse availability and exceptions. Inventory snapshots are not necessarily complete histories of quantities originally received against a GRN. Manual changes and approvals must comply with company authorization procedures.

07. Availability, integrity and audit records

The Platform is under active development. Service interruptions, delayed updates, caching, data import failures and calculation defects can occur. Users should retain original source reports in approved systems and verify that a cloud upload reports success. The organization may maintain operational logs and report-version metadata where required for support, security, audit and applicable retention obligations.

08. Security incidents and investigations

Suspected account compromise, unauthorized access, incorrect data disclosure and malicious uploads must be reported promptly to the designated company administrator or security contact. The organization may investigate suspected misuse in accordance with law, employment policy and any applicable notice requirements. Users must preserve relevant evidence and avoid modifying logs or records during an investigation.

09. Confidentiality and company ownership

The Platform interface, company reports, business methods, and related material remain subject to their respective owners' intellectual-property and confidentiality rights. Unless otherwise authorized, employees receive limited, revocable access for their duties and must return or delete exported copies when directed, subject to required retention.

10. Hosting and third-party services

Website code may be delivered using GitHub Pages, while authenticated accounts, report metadata and private file storage are supported by Supabase. The organizations providing those services have their own terms. Users must not upload actual business spreadsheets into a public source-code repository or share signed download links outside the intended audience.

11. Limitations and responsibility

The Platform is provided as an evolving internal operations tool. The organization makes no promise that every report, chart, count or recommendation is complete or error-free. Nothing in these terms excludes obligations or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Any liability allocation, governing law or dispute process must be reviewed and approved by the organization's legal team before being adopted.

12. Changes, suspension and termination

Platform features and access rules may change following company approval. Where reasonably possible, staff will be informed of material revisions and of any new mandatory acknowledgement. Access may be suspended while a security issue, maintenance event or data integrity incident is investigated.
